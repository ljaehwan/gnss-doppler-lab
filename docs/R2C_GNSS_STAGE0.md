# R2C-GNSS Stage-0 independent-review correction

The corrected status is `DATA_INVALID`, derived from the pre-existing decision taxonomy. Its exact gate reason is `complex_provenance=PASS, time_alignment=NOT_EVALUATED, los_geometry=NOT_EVALUATED, b0_interface=FAIL, clean_dynamic_fpr=NOT_EVALUATED, gain_invariance=NOT_EVALUATED, phase_invariance=NOT_EVALUATED, full_exceeds_b0=NOT_EVALUATED, full_b0_ci=NOT_EVALUATED, geometry_improvement=NOT_EVALUATED, relation_destruction=NOT_EVALUATED, shortcut_controls=NOT_EVALUATED`. `NOT_EVALUATED` means evaluation stopped at the required B0 failure; it does not assert that time alignment or LOS geometry was independently unavailable. No corrected attack metric is reported.

The frozen checkpoint was trained on per-PRN one-second node windows at 0.5-second stride. Each of its nine inputs is an aggregated `tap_*_rel_prompt_mean`; twelve prior windows predict the next window, whose score is available at the end of that one-second window. The rejected implementation instead supplied the first raw receiver epoch in each 0.5-second bin and reused historical standardization and thresholds. Those scores and all Full-versus-B0 comparisons are scientifically invalid. The checkpoint, training summary, training implementation, and historical evaluator are now hash-bound. Raw-row substitution is prohibited, and B0 is `UNAVAILABLE`.

The runner validates the exact seven-scenario roster before fitting or scoring. It requires a nonempty authoritative recording identity and raw-IQ hash, validates the NPZ and export manifest hashes, receiver config and manifest chain, declared shapes, finite nondegenerate I/Q, PRNs, and causal per-channel times/sample counts. cleanDynamic and DS8 identities are resolved only through their pre-existing receiver/upstream manifests; no CLI-name fallback is accepted.

Condition imputation, centering, and scaling are fit only on cleanStatic `normal_train`, then frozen, serialized, hashed, and reused unchanged. Proper complex covariance uses `E[(z-mu)(z-mu)^H]`. Geometry requires at least five PRNs, rank four, positive residual degrees of freedom, and acceptable conditioning; four-PRN events are unavailable. Receiver PVT selection is causal—future fixes are rejected. Geometry fitting also rejects an unauthenticated recording start rather than rounding decoded end-of-run ephemeris time.

Synthetic second-source `power_ratio` now means injected nine-tap energy divided by authentic nine-tap energy. The delayed template is normalized to that authentic reference. Synthetic controls remain mechanics-only and cannot establish attack performance.

The quantitative acceptance rules are genuinely pre-existing in section 11 of the original preregistration. They are encoded in machine-readable config/gates, and the verdict is derived programmatically. Because a required B0 input/interface is unavailable, the preregistered `DATA_INVALID` category applies before attack scoring. This result does not claim that authentic complex data are globally absent, does not report stale A1/A2/A3/A4/Full/B0 metrics, and does not justify a later raw-IQ 2-D model.
