## Signal conditions

Each 30-second geometry has four matched conditions:

1. clean normal;
2. independent PRN-specific multipath;
3. carrier-coupled 100 m carry-off;
4. 100 m code carry-off with counterfeit carrier range/rate locked to authentic.

The attack moves during 5--10 s and evaluation begins at 12 s. Independent
counterfeit carrier phases are seeded by PRN. Normal, coupled, and locked RF
prefixes are byte-identical through attack onset.

## Data retention

Full final artifacts are under
`/home/ubuntu/hdd_data/cgc_temporal_final_static_v1`. Intermediate component
and combined IQ files were hash-checked and removed after successful receiver
runs; retained receiver, truth, log, and analysis outputs occupy about 1.5 GB.
The compact committed record preserves the analysis CSV hashes. See
[`../data/`](../data/) for all external and local locations.

Do not casually rerun this final campaign. A new run is a new experiment version
and must not overwrite the frozen `v1` output root or result summary.
